Controls

Click a key to rebind it, then press the new key (Esc cancels). Each action has a main key and an alternate. Laptop without a numpad? Player 2 can use the alternates K L ; O P.

Combat tips — Press Light up to 3 times for a combo string · Crouching ducks under high punches · Crouch + Light = low sweep ·Crouch + Heavy = launcher uppercut · Attack in the air for jump-ins · Hold Block to reduce damage to chip · Special has a cooldown · Ultimate charges as you deal and take damage.
